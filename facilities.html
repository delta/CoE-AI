Lab Infrastructure

Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (COE–AI Lab) facilities at NIT Tiruchirappalli.

Lab under scaling. Infrastructure and facilities details will be updated as expansion continues.

Facilities

1. High-Performance Computing System
  • System: NVIDIA DGX-1 Server
  • GPU Configuration:
  • 8 × NVIDIA Tesla V100 GPUs
  • 32 GB HBM2 memory per GPU
  • Total GPU Memory: 256 GB
  • CPU: Dual Intel Xeon Processors
  • System Memory: 512 GB
  • Interconnect: NVLink GPU interconnect
  • Storage: High-speed SSD storage array
  • Operating System: DGX OS Server (Ubuntu-based)
  • Purpose: Large-scale AI model training, simulation, and research computing
2. Workstation Cluster
  • Number of Workstations: 6
  • Configuration (per workstation):
  • Intel Core i7-9700 Processor
  • 16 GB DDR4 RAM (3200 MHz)
  • 256 GB SSD + 2 TB HDD
  • NVIDIA RTX 2080 Ti GPU (11 GB GDDR6)
  • Windows 10 Pro (64-bit)
  • Purpose: Data preprocessing, model development, visualization, and inference
3. Power Backup System
  • UPS: 20 kVA Industrial Online UPS
  • Battery Bank:
  • Amaron Quanta 12 V 75 Ah SMF-VRLA batteries
  • Batteries available as on verification: 14 units
  • Backup Duration: Approximately 2–3 hours under full load
  • Purpose: Uninterrupted power supply for DGX-1 server and workstation cluster

Infrastructure Location

All infrastructure is housed in the
Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (COE–AI Lab)
